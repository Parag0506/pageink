# PageInk brand colours

| Use                                    | Hex       | RGB           |
| -------------------------------------- | --------- | ------------- |
| Ink: every line, the "@", the wordmark | `#2f241c` | 47, 36, 28    |
| Page: the sheet                        | `#f8efdc` | 248, 239, 220 |
| Fold: the underside of the corner      | `#e4cfa9` | 228, 207, 169 |
| Accent: the quill                      | `#c98c5e` | 201, 140, 94  |

The cream icons in `png/` sit on the page colour, `#f8efdc`. At 16 and 32 pixels use the
small mark (the sheet, the fold and the "@", heavier lines, no quill); from 48 up use the
full mark.

Files: `pageink-logo.svg` (mark and wordmark, letters as outlines), `pageink-mark.svg`,
`pageink-mark-small.svg`, `underline.svg`, and `png/pageink-mark-<size>-<transparent|cream>.png`
at 16, 32, 48, 128, 256, 512 and 1024. They are kept as files; see `README.md`.
