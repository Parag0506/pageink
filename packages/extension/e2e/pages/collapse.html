Field notes on hedgerows

Hedgerows mark old field edges. Some are older than the villages beside them, and a count of the shrubs in a stretch gives a rough guess at their age.

Plants of the hedge bank

The bank under a hedge holds primroses in spring and marigold seedlings in damp corners, sheltered from the wind by the stems above.

Birds use the hedge as a road between woods. Blackbirds and wrens nest low, while finches feed on the seed heads in winter.
