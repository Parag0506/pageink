Iron gall ink was the standard writing ink in Europe for over a thousand years. It bites into the paper, so a pomegranate seed of ink can last as long as the sheet itself.

Pencils came later. Graphite from Borrowdale was so pure that it could be sawn into sticks and wrapped in wood.
